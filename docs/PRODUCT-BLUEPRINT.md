# CAPITAL//CONSTITUTION — Product Blueprint

## Category

Enterprise AI Economic Authority Layer

## Core object: Capital Contract

A Capital Contract is a machine-readable instrument binding an AI workload to:

- accountable business and risk owners
- approved capital ceiling
- required value-to-cost ratio
- quality, latency and control floors
- approved model and vendor mix
- autonomous authority level
- automatic cure actions
- suspension and return-to-human-authority conditions
- expiration and renewal evidence

## Defensible data moat

1. **AI Economic Graph** — request → model → agent action → workflow → customer/product → verified outcome → financial value.
2. **Elasticity History** — observed response of quality, cost and latency to routing, prompts, context, retries, caching and adoption.
3. **Decision Memory** — what management funded, constrained, rejected or waived and the result of those decisions.
4. **Policy in the Transaction Path** — authority is enforced before spend occurs, not explained after invoicing.
5. **Benchmark Network** — privacy-preserving unit-economics benchmarks by workload and industry.

- Identity and role-based authority
- Provider and cloud cost ingestion

- Approval workflow

## Three-stage autonomy model

### Stage 1 — Advisory
Observe, attribute, forecast and recommend.

### Stage 2 — Approval-based execution
Prepare routing, budget and containment actions for accountable approval.

### Stage 3 — Bounded autonomy
Execute pre-authorized cures within spend, quality and risk boundaries; fail closed when authority is unclear.
